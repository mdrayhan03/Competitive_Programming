## 12. FORMATTING RULES FOR THE EXPORTED PDF
> ATS parsers break on layout before they break on content.

1. **Single column.** Your current two-column layout risks the parser reading the sidebar mid-sentence. Move Skills / Education / Awards into full-width sections stacked below Experience.
2. **No tables, text boxes, headers, footers or graphics.** Contact details go in the body, not a header.
3. **Standard section headings** the parser recognises: `Professional Summary`, `Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`, `Projects`, `Certifications`.
4. **Dates in one consistent format:** `Dec 2025 – Present`. Month + year, every time.
5. **One standard font** (Calibri, Arial, Georgia), 10–11.5pt, black text.
6. **Export to PDF, never a scan or image.** Then open the PDF, select all, copy, paste into a plain text file — if it reads in the wrong order, the parser will too.
7. **File name:** `MD Rayhan Hossain_<Exact Job Title>_Resume.pdf`.
8. **Bullets start with a verb, and two per role carry a number.** Built, Designed, Cut, Automated, Integrated, Architected, Migrated, Reduced.
